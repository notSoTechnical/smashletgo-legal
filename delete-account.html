Delete your Smash & Let Go data

Smash & Let Go is made by Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC. Support: info@annizon.com.

There is no account to delete

Smash & Let Go has no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. Everything — your plate balance, settings, and your Broken Journal — lives only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive it and cannot delete it for you, because we never had it.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

Open Smash & Let Go → Settings → Erase everything. This immediately removes your plate balance, settings, and Broken Journal from the device. Deleting the app itself removes all of it too.

Before you erase

Your Broken Journal export is free in the app — Journal → Share — export first if you want to keep the record; after erasing it cannot be recovered.
