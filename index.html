What Smash & Let Go does

The only plate-smashing app where you write what you are letting go of — or celebrating — on the plate before you throw it, with two modes no other smasher has: Let Go and Opa Celebration. Write a word or phrase on a virtual plate, flick it at the wall, watch it shatter, sweep up, and keep a Broken Journal of everything you smashed. For entertainment only. Not a treatment for anger or mental health.

Common questions

How do I play?
Write on the plate, pick a plate type, then flick the plate up toward the wall to throw it. Tap Sweep up after it shatters to save it in your Broken Journal.

How many free plates do I get?
10 free plates every day. Extra plates are available in plate packs in the Shop.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything stays on your device.

How do I delete my data?
In the app: Settings → Erase everything. Full instructions are on the data deletion page.
