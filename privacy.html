Smash & Let Go — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

Smash & Let Go is a plate-smashing entertainment game that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no server of ours, and no sign-in.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your plate balance, your Broken Journal (the words you wrote on plates, dates, modes and plate types), and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them, cannot see them, and cannot restore them. Deleting the app, or erasing everything in Settings, removes all of it.

BUYING PLATE PACKS

Plate packs are sold through the App Store / Google Play and processed for us by RevenueCat, our in-app purchase provider. When you buy a pack, Apple/Google and RevenueCat handle the payment and receive the purchase information they need (such as the product bought and a transaction identifier). We never see your payment card. RevenueCat's own privacy policy describes its processing. Nothing about your journal or play is sent anywhere.

NO ANALYTICS, NO ADS, NO TRACKING

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. There is no AI processing.

CHILDREN

The game is rated 9+ (mild cartoon destruction). Because the app collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either. Purchases are made through the store account on the device, under the store's family and parental controls.
