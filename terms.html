Smash & Let Go — Terms of Use

Effective: November 17, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

THE GAME

Smash & Let Go is an entertainment game: you write a word or phrase on a virtual plate, throw it at a virtual wall, watch it shatter, sweep up, and keep a Broken Journal of what you smashed. For entertainment only. Not a treatment for anger or mental health.

VIRTUAL ITEMS

Plates are virtual items that exist only inside the game on your device. You get 10 free plates every day. Extra plates can be bought in plate packs through the store. Virtual items have no cash value, are not transferable, and are not refundable by us — store purchases are governed by the store's own refund policy. If you erase the app's data or delete the app, unspent plates are lost, because there is no account to hold them.

FAIR PLAY

The rules are the same for everyone: one plate per throw, the daily grant lands once per calendar day, and gold / patterned plates in Opa mode are unlocked by how many plates you have smashed in total, never only by paying.

AVAILABILITY

The game runs on your device and needs no connection except to buy plate packs. We may update the game over time; saved journals carry forward where we reasonably can.

LIABILITY

The game is provided as-is for entertainment. Sounds in the game are synthesized, not recordings of real plates.
